---
title: Crosswind
summary: Wind blowing across the hole — aim into the wind and let it bring the ball back.
category: conditions
tags: [wind, aim, shot-shaping]
aliases: [side wind, wind from the left, wind from the right]
level: all
clubs: [driver, woods, hybrids, irons, wedges]
setup:
  - Check the wind at your position and in the treetops near the green.
  - Aim upwind of the target so the wind drifts the ball back.
  - Make a normal setup; let your aim do the work.
swing:
  - Swing smoothly with your normal shot shape.
  - Keep the ball flight a little lower to reduce how much it drifts.
expect:
  - The ball drifts with the wind and runs on in that direction after landing.
  - Longer clubs and higher shots drift more.
mistakes:
  - Aiming straight at the flag and watching the ball blow off the green.
  - Under-estimating drift on high-lofted shots.
club_choice: A crosswind usually costs some distance too — allow about a half club extra in a strong wind.
advanced: Better players can shape the ball into the wind (a draw into a {left}-to-{right} wind) to hold the line.
related: [into-the-wind, downwind, intentional-draw, intentional-fade]
viz: crosswind
updated: 2026-10-31
