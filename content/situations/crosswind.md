---
title: Crosswind
summary: Wind blowing across the hole — aim into the wind and let it bring the ball back.
category: conditions
tags: [wind, aim, shot-shaping]
aliases: [side wind, wind from the left, wind from the right]
level: all
clubs: [driver, woods, hybrids, irons, wedges]
setup:
  - Check the wind here and in the treetops by the green.
  - Aim upwind and let the wind bring it back.
  - Set up normally; let your aim do the work.
swing:
  - Swing smoothly with your normal shape.
  - Keep the flight a little lower.
expect:
  - The ball drifts and runs downwind after landing.
  - Longer clubs and higher shots drift more.
mistakes:
  - Aiming straight at the flag.
  - Underestimating drift on lofted shots.
club_choice: Add about half a club in a strong crosswind.
advanced: Shape the ball into the wind to hold its line.
related: [into-the-wind, downwind, intentional-draw, intentional-fade]
viz: crosswind
updated: 2026-10-31
