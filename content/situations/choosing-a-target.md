---
title: Choosing a target on approach shots
summary: Aim for the safe part of the green, usually the middle, rather than at every flag.
category: strategy
tags: [course-management, aim, club-selection]
aliases: [aim for the middle of the green, flag hunting, where to aim on approach]
level: all
clubs: [irons, hybrids, wedges]
setup:
  - Find the trouble and the big, safe area.
  - Aim at the middle of the green unless the flag is easy.
  - Club for the middle, not the flag.
swing:
  - Commit to the target once chosen.
  - Make your normal swing.
expect:
  - More greens hit and fewer big numbers.
mistakes:
  - Aiming at a flag tucked by trouble.
  - Clubbing for a perfect strike.
fixes:
  - Aim for the middle of the green, away from the trouble.
  - Club for your typical shot, so a slight miss still finds the green.
club_choice: Between clubs, take the one that stays away from the trouble.
related: [laying-up, flyer-lie, downwind, intentional-draw, intentional-fade]
updated: 2026-11-03
---

Most amateurs come up short far more often than long. Plan for your typical shot, not your best one.
