---
title: Low shot (punch or knockdown)
summary: Keep the ball low to stay under wind or branches, using a shorter swing and a lower-lofted club.
category: trajectory
tags: [trajectory, ball-position, wind, club-selection]
aliases: [punch shot, knockdown shot, keeping it low]
level: all
clubs: [irons, hybrids]
setup:
  - Take one or two clubs more.
  - Play the ball back of center.
  - Grip down and set your hands ahead.
  - Favor your lead side.
swing:
  - Make a smooth three-quarter backswing.
  - Keep your hands ahead through impact.
  - Finish low, about chest height.
expect:
  - Lower flight with more roll.
  - Less spin, so less wind effect.
mistakes:
  - Swinging hard, which makes it climb.
  - Flipping the wrists at impact.
club_choice: More club, less effort.
drill: Hit 7-irons finishing with your hands below your shoulders.
advanced: A ball's width farther back flies noticeably lower.
related: [high-shot, into-the-wind, punch-out-from-trees, divot-lie]
viz: low-shot
updated: 2026-10-31
---
