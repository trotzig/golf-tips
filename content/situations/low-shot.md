---
title: Low shot (punch or knockdown)
summary: Keep the ball low to stay under wind or branches, using a shorter swing and a lower-lofted club.
category: trajectory
tags: [trajectory, ball-position, wind, club-selection]
aliases: [punch shot, knockdown shot, keeping it low]
level: all
clubs: [irons, hybrids]
setup:
  - Take one or two clubs more than the distance needs.
  - Play the ball back of center, toward your trail foot.
  - Grip down an inch or two and set your hands slightly ahead of the ball.
  - Put a little more weight on your lead side.
swing:
  - Make a three-quarter backswing with a smooth tempo.
  - Lead with your hands through impact so they stay ahead of the clubhead.
  - Finish with a short, low follow-through, about chest height.
expect:
  - Lower flight with more roll after landing.
  - Less spin, so the ball is less affected by wind.
mistakes:
  - Swinging hard; extra speed adds spin and makes the ball climb.
  - Flipping the wrists at impact, which adds loft back.
club_choice: More club, less effort — a smooth swing with a longer club keeps the ball down.
drill: Hit balls with a 7-iron and try to finish with your hands no higher than your shoulders.
advanced: Vary height by ball position alone — a ball's width farther back gives a noticeably lower flight.
related: [high-shot, into-the-wind, punch-out-from-trees, divot-lie]
viz: low-shot
