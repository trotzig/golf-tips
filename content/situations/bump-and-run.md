---
title: Bump-and-run
summary: Low running shot with a less-lofted club — carry it a short way and let it roll like a putt.
category: short-game
tags: [chipping, short-game, distance-control, wind]
aliases: [chip and run, running chip, low chip with an iron]
level: all
clubs: [irons, hybrids]
setup:
  - Pick a 7-, 8- or 9-iron for the roll you need.
  - Set up like a putt, ball back, grip down.
  - Keep your weight on your lead side.
swing:
  - Make a putting stroke with quiet wrists.
  - Land it just on the green or firm ground short of it.
expect:
  - A short flight and a long roll.
  - Consistent results, especially in wind or on firm ground.
mistakes:
  - Landing the ball in long grass.
  - Flicking the wrists.
club_choice: Use the least loft that clears the fringe.
advanced: A hybrid works the same way from the fringe.
related: [basic-chip, chip-or-putt, putting-from-the-fringe, tight-lie, into-the-wind]
viz: bump-and-run
updated: 2026-10-31
---

A long-standing rule of thumb: get the ball rolling as soon as you can. The lower the shot, the
more predictable the result.
