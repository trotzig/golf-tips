---
title: Bump-and-run
summary: Low running shot with a less-lofted club — carry it a short way and let it roll like a putt.
category: short-game
tags: [chipping, short-game, distance-control, wind]
aliases: [chip and run, running chip, low chip with an iron]
level: all
clubs: [irons, hybrids]
setup:
  - Pick a 7-, 8- or 9-iron, depending on how far the ball needs to roll.
  - Set up like a putt — narrow stance, ball back of center, grip down the shaft.
  - Keep your weight on your lead side.
swing:
  - Use a putting-style stroke, rocking the shoulders with quiet wrists.
  - Land the ball just onto the green or on firm fairway in front of it.
expect:
  - A short flight and a long roll toward the hole.
  - Very consistent results, especially in wind or on firm ground.
mistakes:
  - Landing the ball in long grass, which stops it unpredictably.
  - Using a wrist flick that adds height.
club_choice: Choose the least lofted club that will clear the fringe, then let it roll.
advanced: A hybrid can be used the same way from the fringe; the wide sole helps it glide through grass.
related: [basic-chip, chip-or-putt, putting-from-the-fringe, tight-lie, into-the-wind]
updated: 2026-10-31
---

A long-standing rule of thumb: get the ball rolling as soon as you can. The lower the shot, the
more predictable the result.
