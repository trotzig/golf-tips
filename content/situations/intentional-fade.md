---
title: Intentional fade
summary: Curve the ball gently from {left} to {right} on purpose for a softer, more controlled shot.
category: trajectory
tags: [shot-shaping, aim, alignment]
aliases: [hitting a fade, "curving the ball {right}", "{left} to {right} shot"]
level: advanced
clubs: [driver, woods, hybrids, irons]
setup:
  - Aim your body {left} of the target.
  - Point the face at the target.
  - Set the face, then grip.
  - Ball in normal position or a fraction forward.
swing:
  - Swing along your body line.
  - Hold the face off through impact.
expect:
  - Starts {left}, curves back to the target.
  - Slightly higher, landing softer.
mistakes:
  - Aiming the face with your body — a pull.
  - Opening the face too far — a slice.
fixes:
  - Set the face at the target first, then aim your body {left}.
  - Open the face only enough to point at the target; the stance adds the curve.
club_choice: Consider one club more.
club_change: 1
drill: Start balls along an alignment stick on your body line.
related: [intentional-draw, crosswind, ball-below-feet, choosing-a-target]

---

Only try a shaped shot on the course once you can hit it reliably in practice. If in doubt,
aim for the widest safe area and play your normal shot.
