---
title: Recovering after a bad hole
summary: Just made a big number — let it go, reset, and play the next hole with a simple, safe plan.
category: mental
tags: [mental, course-management, routine]
aliases: [after a double bogey, bouncing back from a blow-up hole, staying calm after a bad shot]
level: all
setup:
  - Give yourself until the next tee to be annoyed, then let it go.
  - Take a few slow breaths and loosen your grip and shoulders.
  - Pick a conservative target and a club you trust for the next tee shot.
swing:
  - Commit fully to your normal pre-shot routine.
  - Swing with smooth tempo; don't try to win the strokes back in one shot.
expect:
  - One bad hole rarely ruins a round — chasing it often does.
mistakes:
  - Trying risky "revenge" shots to make up lost strokes.
  - Replaying the bad hole in your head while playing the next.
club_choice: Play the next hole for a solid par or bogey, not a birdie to cancel it out.
advanced: Set a small goal for the next three holes, such as hitting every fairway or green with a full routine.
related: [first-tee-nerves, pre-shot-routine, laying-up, punch-out-from-trees]
updated: 2026-10-31
