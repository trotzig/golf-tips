---
title: Recovering after a bad hole
summary: Just made a big number — let it go, reset, and play the next hole with a simple, safe plan.
category: mental
tags: [mental, course-management, routine]
aliases: [after a double bogey, bouncing back from a blow-up hole, staying calm after a bad shot]
level: all
setup:
  - Be annoyed until the next tee, then let it go.
  - Take a few slow breaths; loosen grip and shoulders.
  - Pick a safe target and a club you trust.
swing:
  - Commit to your normal routine.
  - Swing smoothly; don't chase lost strokes in one shot.
expect:
  - One bad hole rarely ruins a round — chasing it often does.
mistakes:
  - Risky "revenge" shots to win strokes back.
  - Replaying the bad hole in your head.
club_choice: Play for a solid par or bogey, not a make-up birdie.
advanced: Set a small goal for the next three holes, like a full routine every shot.
related: [first-tee-nerves, pre-shot-routine, laying-up, punch-out-from-trees]
updated: 2026-10-31
---
