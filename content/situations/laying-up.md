---
title: Laying up
summary: When reaching the green is risky, play short to a spot that leaves a comfortable next shot.
category: strategy
tags: [course-management, club-selection, recovery]
aliases: [lay up short of water, should I go for the green, playing safe]
level: all
clubs: [irons, hybrids, wedges]
setup:
  - Ask how often you'd really pull off the shot.
  - Under half the time, with costly misses? Lay up.
  - Leave a full swing with a club you like.
  - Pick flat fairway well short of trouble.
swing:
  - Make a smooth, committed swing.
  - Use your full routine; it's a real shot.
expect:
  - One extra shot, far fewer penalties.
mistakes:
  - Leaving an awkward half-wedge.
  - Laying up too close to the hazard.
club_choice: Club for the lay-up target, then swing normally.
advanced: A full wedge from flat ground beats an awkward short pitch.
