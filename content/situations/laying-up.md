---
title: Laying up
summary: When reaching the green is risky, play short to a spot that leaves a comfortable next shot.
category: strategy
tags: [course-management, club-selection, recovery]
aliases: [lay up short of water, should I go for the green, playing safe]
level: all
clubs: [irons, hybrids, wedges]
setup:
  - Ask honestly how often you'd pull off the shot to reach the green.
  - If it's less than about half the time, and missing is costly, lay up.
  - Pick a lay-up distance that leaves a full swing with a club you like.
  - Choose a flat, open part of the fairway, well short of the trouble.
swing:
  - Make a smooth, committed swing at the lay-up target.
  - Treat it as a real shot with your full routine — not a casual tap.
expect:
  - One extra shot to reach the green, but far fewer penalty strokes.
mistakes:
  - Laying up to an awkward half-wedge distance.
  - Laying up so close to the hazard that a slight mishit finds it anyway.
club_choice: Pick the club for the lay-up target first, then make your normal swing with it.
advanced: A full wedge from a flat lie is often easier to hit close than a short pitch from an awkward one.
