---
title: Playing in the rain
summary: Wet conditions — keep grips dry, take more club and expect little roll.
category: conditions
tags: [weather, club-selection, safety]
aliases: [wet weather golf, rainy round, playing on a wet course]
level: all
clubs: [driver, woods, hybrids, irons, wedges, putter]
setup:
  - Dry your grips and hands before each shot.
  - Wear rain gloves if you have them.
  - Make sure of your footing; wet ground is slippery.
swing:
  - Make a slightly shorter swing for balance.
  - Pick the ball clean; wet turf grabs heavy strikes.
expect:
  - Shorter carry, much less roll.
  - Slower greens, firmer bunker sand.
mistakes:
  - Letting grips get wet.
  - Hurrying to get out of the rain.
club_choice: One more club; fly it to the target.
advanced: Putt firmer on wet greens; expect less break.
related: [cold-weather, into-the-wind, chip-or-putt]
updated: 2026-10-31
---

Safety first: if there is thunder or lightning, stop play at once and get to a safe building or
vehicle. Don't shelter under trees or hold an umbrella or club in the open. Under the Rules of
Golf, you can take free relief from temporary water (puddles) on the course.
