---
title: First-tee nerves
summary: Jittery on the opening tee shot — slow your breathing, pick a safe target and swing a club you trust.
category: mental
tags: [mental, nerves, routine, tee-shot]
aliases: [nervous on the first tee, people watching me tee off, opening tee shot anxiety]
level: all
clubs: [driver, woods, hybrids, irons]
setup:
  - Arrive early enough to warm up.
  - Hit a club you trust, not necessarily driver.
  - Pick a specific target on the safe side.
swing:
  - Take two slow breaths, exhaling longer than you inhale.
  - Use your normal routine at its normal pace.
  - Swing at about 80% with a full finish.
expect:
  - Nerves are normal; any shot in play is a success.
mistakes:
  - Swinging harder to impress.
  - Thinking mechanics instead of target.
club_choice: Take the club that keeps it in play.
drill: End your warm-up by rehearsing the first tee shot.
related: [pre-shot-routine, after-a-bad-hole, choosing-a-target, short-putts]
updated: 2026-10-31
---

Everyone watching is more worried about their own shot than yours. Focus on what you can control:
your breathing, your routine and your target.
