---
title: First-tee nerves
summary: Jittery on the opening tee shot — slow your breathing, pick a safe target and swing a club you trust.
category: mental
tags: [mental, nerves, routine, tee-shot]
aliases: [nervous on the first tee, people watching me tee off, opening tee shot anxiety]
level: all
clubs: [driver, woods, hybrids, irons]
setup:
  - Arrive early enough to warm up, so you're not rushing to the tee.
  - Choose a club you hit reliably; it doesn't have to be the driver.
  - Pick a wide, specific target and the safest side of the fairway.
swing:
  - Take two slow, deep breaths before you step in, exhaling longer than you inhale.
  - Go through your normal pre-shot routine, at its normal pace.
  - Swing at about 80% effort with a full finish.
expect:
  - Nerves are normal, even for professionals; a solid, in-play shot is a success.
mistakes:
  - Swinging harder to impress, which usually leads to a big miss.
  - Thinking about swing mechanics instead of the target.
club_choice: Take the club that keeps the ball in play, even if it leaves a longer second shot.
drill: On the range, finish your warm-up by hitting the first tee shot — same club, same target, full routine.
related: [pre-shot-routine, after-a-bad-hole, choosing-a-target, short-putts]
updated: 2026-10-31
---

Everyone watching is more worried about their own shot than yours. Focus on what you can control:
your breathing, your routine and your target.
