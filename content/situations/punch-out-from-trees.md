---
title: Punching out from trees
summary: Blocked by trees — take your medicine with a low punch back to the fairway instead of the hero shot.
category: rough-and-trouble
tags: [recovery, trajectory, course-management, safety]
aliases: [stuck behind a tree, chip out sideways, ball in the woods]
level: all
clubs: [irons, hybrids]
setup:
  - Find the widest, lowest gap, even sideways.
  - Check branches for your swing.
  - Take a 6- or 7-iron to keep it low.
  - Ball back, hands ahead.
swing:
  - Make a short backswing, waist to chest high.
  - Keep your hands ahead; hold a low finish.
expect:
  - A low shot that runs a long way.
  - Usually costs just one stroke.
mistakes:
  - Threading a narrow gap toward the green.
  - Hitting so hard it runs into more trouble.
club_choice: Take the club that stays under the branches; swing gently.
advanced: Leave a full swing with your favorite club.
related: [low-shot, thick-rough, laying-up, after-a-bad-hole]

---

Safety first: balls can rebound hard off trunks and branches. Choose a gap well away from the
nearest trunk and make sure no one is standing in the trees nearby.
