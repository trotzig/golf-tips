---
title: Putting from the fringe
summary: Ball on the short grass just off the green — the putter is often the safest club.
category: putting
tags: [putting, speed-control, chipping]
aliases: [putting from the apron, putting from the collar, ball on the fringe]
level: all
clubs: [putter, hybrids]
setup:
  - Check the grass between ball and green; putt if it's short and lies with the ball.
  - Set up as for a normal putt, eyes over the ball.
  - Read the putt from the green's edge as well as from the ball.
swing:
  - Make your normal stroke, just a little longer to get through the fringe.
  - Keep the putter low to the ground so the ball rolls rather than hops.
expect:
  - The ball slows in the fringe, then rolls normally once on the green.
mistakes:
  - Underestimating the fringe and leaving the putt well short.
  - Putting through grass that grows against the putt or is wet; chip instead.
club_choice: Putt when the fringe is short and dry; if it's too long, a bump-and-run or a hybrid "chip-putt" works well.
drill: From the fringe, putt to a target and note how much longer the stroke needs to be than from the green.
related: [chip-or-putt, bump-and-run, lag-putting]
updated: 2026-10-31
