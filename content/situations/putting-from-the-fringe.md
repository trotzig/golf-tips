---
title: Putting from the fringe
summary: Ball on the short grass just off the green — the putter is often the safest club.
category: putting
tags: [putting, speed-control, chipping]
aliases: [putting from the apron, putting from the collar, ball on the fringe]
level: all
clubs: [putter, hybrids]
setup:
  - Putt if the fringe is short and grows with the putt.
  - Set up as for a normal putt.
  - Read it from the green's edge too.
swing:
  - Make your normal stroke, a little longer.
  - Keep the putter low so the ball rolls.
expect:
  - The ball slows in the fringe, then rolls normally.
mistakes:
  - Leaving it short.
  - Putting through wet or into-the-grain grass.
club_choice: Putt if short and dry; otherwise bump-and-run or hybrid.
drill: Learn how much longer your stroke must be from the fringe.
related: [chip-or-putt, bump-and-run, lag-putting]
updated: 2026-10-31
---
