---
title: Flyer lie
summary: Ball sitting up in light rough — grass trapped at impact cuts spin, so the ball flies farther and rolls more.
category: rough-and-trouble
tags: [rough, spin, club-selection]
aliases: [flier lie, ball flying too far from the rough, jumper from the rough]
level: all
clubs: [irons, wedges]
setup:
  - Look for a ball perched up in light rough.
  - Set up as from the fairway.
  - Plan to land short and let it run.
swing:
  - Make a smooth, normal swing.
expect:
  - Less spin; it flies farther and won't stop.
  - Less predictable distance.
mistakes:
  - Taking your normal club and flying the green.
  - Attacking a flag tucked behind a bunker.
fixes:
  - Take one club less and plan for the ball to run.
  - Aim for the middle of the green and let it release.
club_choice: One club less, or swing softer.
club_change: -1
related: [thick-rough, choosing-a-target]
viz: flyer-lie
updated: 2026-11-03
