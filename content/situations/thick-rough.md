---
title: Thick rough
summary: Ball sitting down in long grass — take more loft, hit down firmly and focus on getting back in play.
category: rough-and-trouble
tags: [rough, recovery, club-selection, contact]
aliases: [deep rough, ball buried in long grass, heavy rough]
level: all
clubs: [irons, wedges, hybrids]
setup:
  - Choose a lofted club.
  - Play the ball slightly back.
  - Grip a little firmer.
  - Aim for the safest open area.
swing:
  - Set your wrists early for a steeper swing.
  - Hit down firmly and swing through the grass.
expect:
  - Less spin; the ball runs out more.
  - Grass can close the face, sending it {left}.
mistakes:
  - Taking a long club to reach the green.
  - Decelerating into the grass.
fixes:
  - Take a lofted club and get back to the fairway.
  - Grip firmer and accelerate through the grass to a finish.
club_choice: Take the loft that gets you safely out.
related: [flyer-lie, punch-out-from-trees, laying-up, high-shot]
viz: thick-rough
updated: 2026-11-03
---

The goal is the next shot. One clean escape to the fairway beats two attempts to reach the green.
