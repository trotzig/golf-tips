---
title: High shot
summary: Hit the ball higher to carry an obstacle or stop it quickly on the green.
category: trajectory
tags: [trajectory, ball-position, club-selection]
aliases: [hitting it higher, getting over a tree, high soft shot]
level: all
clubs: [irons, wedges, hybrids]
setup:
  - Choose enough loft; the club does the work.
  - Play the ball a ball's width forward.
  - Widen your stance slightly, weight centered.
swing:
  - Make a full, unhurried swing to a high finish.
  - Swing down through; let the loft lift it.
expect:
  - Steeper landing and less roll.
  - Less distance and more wind effect.
mistakes:
  - Hanging back to scoop the ball.
  - Not checking the loft clears the obstacle.
fixes:
  - Stay centered and swing down through; let the loft lift the ball.
  - Choose a club whose loft clearly clears the obstacle.
club_choice: Take the club that clears it; accept the distance.
related: [low-shot, lob-shot, punch-out-from-trees, thick-rough]
viz: high-shot
updated: 2026-11-03
---

To check if a club will clear a tree, set it down behind the ball, then gently step on the face so
it lies flat, face up. The shaft now points roughly along the ball's launch angle.
