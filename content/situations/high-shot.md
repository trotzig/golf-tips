---
title: High shot
summary: Hit the ball higher to carry an obstacle or stop it quickly on the green.
category: trajectory
tags: [trajectory, ball-position, club-selection]
aliases: [hitting it higher, getting over a tree, high soft shot]
level: all
clubs: [irons, wedges, hybrids]
setup:
  - Choose enough loft first — the club does most of the work.
  - Play the ball about a ball's width forward of normal.
  - Widen your stance slightly and keep your weight balanced, not leaning toward the target.
swing:
  - Make a full, unhurried swing and turn through to a high finish.
  - Let the club's loft lift the ball; keep swinging down through it.
expect:
  - Steeper landing and less roll.
  - Slightly less distance, and the ball is more affected by wind.
mistakes:
  - Hanging back and trying to scoop the ball up, which causes thin or fat shots.
  - Underestimating the obstacle — check you can clear it with the loft you have.
club_choice: Pick the club that clears the obstacle comfortably, then accept the distance it gives you.
advanced: Open the face slightly and hold it open through impact for extra height; it also adds a touch of fade.
related: [low-shot, lob-shot, punch-out-from-trees, thick-rough]
viz: high-shot
updated: 2026-10-31
---

To check if a club will clear a tree, set it down behind the ball, then gently step on the face so
it lies flat, face up. The shaft now points roughly along the ball's launch angle.
