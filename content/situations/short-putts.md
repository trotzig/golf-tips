---
title: Short putts
summary: Putts from about three to six feet — pick a line, commit, and hit it firmly enough to hold that line.
category: putting
tags: [putting, green-reading, mental, nerves]
aliases: [missing short putts, three foot putts, putting yips]
level: all
setup:
  - Read it briefly; short putts break less.
  - Pick a precise target on the hole.
  - Eyes over the ball, face square to your line.
swing:
  - Make a smooth, compact stroke.
  - Keep your head still until the ball is gone.
  - Roll it firmly enough to go a little past.
expect:
  - A firm stroke takes out much of the break.
mistakes:
  - Looking up early.
  - Decelerating.
club_choice: Commit to one line and speed; don't change over the ball.
drill: Hole a circle of balls at three feet, then move back.
advanced: Listen for the ball to drop instead of watching.
related: [lag-putting, pre-shot-routine, first-tee-nerves]
updated: 2026-10-31
---
