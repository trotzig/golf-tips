---
title: Short putts
summary: Putts from about three to six feet — pick a line, commit, and hit it firmly enough to hold that line.
category: putting
tags: [putting, green-reading, mental, nerves]
aliases: [missing short putts, three foot putts, putting yips]
level: all
setup:
  - Read the putt briefly; short putts break less, especially when hit firmly.
  - Pick a precise target, such as a spot on the back of the hole or the edge of the cup.
  - Set your eyes over or just inside the ball and your putter face square to your line.
swing:
  - Make a smooth, compact stroke with no extra hand action.
  - Keep your head and lower body still until the ball has gone.
  - Strike the ball firmly enough to roll a little past the hole if it misses.
expect:
  - A firm, confident stroke takes much of the break out of a short putt.
mistakes:
  - Looking up early to see if it went in, which pulls the stroke off line.
  - Decelerating, which lets the ball wander off line.
club_choice: Commit to one line — firm and straighter, or softer with more break — and don't change your mind over the ball.
drill: Place balls around the hole at three feet and hole them all in a row before moving back a foot.
advanced: Listen for the ball to drop rather than watching it; it helps keep the head still.
related: [lag-putting, pre-shot-routine, first-tee-nerves]
updated: 2026-10-31
