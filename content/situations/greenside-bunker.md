---
title: Greenside bunker
summary: Splash shot from a bunker next to the green — hit the sand behind the ball, not the ball itself.
category: bunkers
tags: [sand, short-game, contact]
aliases: [sand trap near the green, splash shot, getting out of a bunker]
level: all
clubs: [wedges]
setup:
  - Use a sand wedge and open the face slightly before you take your grip.
  - Take a stance a little wider than shoulder width and wiggle your feet into the sand.
  - Play the ball forward of center, toward your lead heel.
  - Keep a little more weight on your lead side.
swing:
  - Focus on a spot about two inches (5 cm) behind the ball and enter the sand there.
  - Make a fuller swing than you would for a chip of the same distance.
  - Accelerate through the sand to a full finish — the sand carries the ball out.
expect:
  - The ball pops out softly on a cushion of sand and stops fairly quickly.
  - Distance is controlled mostly by swing length, not by how much sand you take.
mistakes:
  - Trying to pick the ball cleanly, which sends it over the green.
  - Slowing down into the sand and leaving the ball in the bunker.
club_choice: Use a sand wedge for most shots; the wide sole (bounce) is designed to glide through sand.
drill: In a practice bunker, draw a line in the sand and practice entering the sand on the line, without a ball.
advanced: For a longer bunker shot, square the face more or use a gap or pitching wedge.
related: [plugged-bunker-lie, fairway-bunker, lob-shot, pitch-shot]
updated: 2026-10-31
---

Under the Rules of Golf, don't touch the sand with your club before the stroke — not at address,
in practice swings, or on the backswing.
