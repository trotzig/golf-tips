---
title: Greenside bunker
summary: Splash shot from a bunker next to the green — hit the sand behind the ball, not the ball itself.
category: bunkers
tags: [sand, short-game, contact]
aliases: [sand trap near the green, splash shot, getting out of a bunker]
level: all
clubs: [wedges]
setup:
  - Open the sand wedge face slightly, then grip.
  - Take a wide stance; settle your feet in the sand.
  - Play the ball forward, toward your lead heel.
  - Favor your lead side.
swing:
  - Enter the sand about two inches (5 cm) behind the ball.
  - Swing longer than for a chip of the same distance.
  - Accelerate through to a full finish.
expect:
  - The ball pops out softly and stops quickly.
  - Swing length controls distance.
mistakes:
  - Picking the ball clean and flying the green.
  - Slowing down into the sand.
club_choice: Use a sand wedge; its bounce glides through sand.
drill: Draw a line in the sand and practice hitting it, no ball.
advanced: For longer shots, square the face or use less loft.
related: [plugged-bunker-lie, fairway-bunker, lob-shot, pitch-shot]
viz: greenside-bunker
updated: 2026-10-31
---

Under the Rules of Golf, don't touch the sand with your club before the stroke — not at address,
in practice swings, or on the backswing.
