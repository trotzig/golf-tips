---
title: Into the wind
summary: Playing against the wind — take more club, swing easier and keep the ball flight down.
category: conditions
tags: [wind, club-selection, trajectory, spin, tee-shot]
aliases: [hitting into a headwind, windy day, playing into the wind]
level: all
clubs: [driver, woods, hybrids, irons, wedges]
setup:
  - Take one or more extra clubs depending on wind strength.
  - Play the ball slightly back of normal.
  - Widen your stance a little for stability.
swing:
  - Swing easier, with smooth tempo; "when it's breezy, swing easy."
  - Make a slightly shorter follow-through to keep the flight lower.
expect:
  - The wind adds spin and makes any curve bigger; a slice or hook is magnified.
  - Shots land more softly and roll less.
mistakes:
  - Swinging harder, which adds spin and sends the ball even higher.
  - Under-clubbing and coming up short of the green.
club_choice: As a rough guide, add one club for every 10 mph (15 km/h) of headwind.
drill: On a windy range, hit the same target with your normal club, then with one more club swung easier; compare.
advanced: Tee the ball slightly lower with the driver to reduce spin and launch.
related: [downwind, crosswind, low-shot, bump-and-run]
updated: 2026-10-31
