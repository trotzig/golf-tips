---
title: Into the wind
summary: Playing against the wind — take more club, swing easier and keep the ball flight down.
category: conditions
tags: [wind, club-selection, trajectory, spin, tee-shot]
aliases: [hitting into a headwind, windy day, playing into the wind]
level: all
clubs: [driver, woods, hybrids, irons, wedges]
setup:
  - Take one or more extra clubs.
  - Play the ball slightly back of normal.
  - Widen your stance a little for stability.
swing:
  - Swing easier — "when it's breezy, swing easy."
  - Shorten the follow-through to keep it low.
expect:
  - Any curve is magnified.
  - Shots land more softly and roll less.
mistakes:
  - Swinging harder, which sends it higher.
  - Under-clubbing and coming up short.
club_choice: Add one club per {10 mph} of headwind.
drill: On a windy range, compare your normal club with one more swung easy.
advanced: Tee the driver slightly lower.
related: [downwind, crosswind, low-shot, bump-and-run]
viz: into-the-wind
