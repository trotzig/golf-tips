---
title: Intentional draw
summary: Curve the ball gently from {right} to {left} on purpose to get around an obstacle or to a flag.
category: trajectory
tags: [shot-shaping, aim, alignment]
aliases: [hitting a draw, "curving the ball {left}", "{right} to {left} shot"]
level: advanced
clubs: [driver, woods, hybrids, irons]
setup:
  - Aim your body {right} of the target.
  - Point the face at the target.
  - Set the face, then grip.
  - Ball in normal position or a fraction back.
swing:
  - Swing along your body line.
  - Let the setup create the curve.
expect:
  - Starts {right}, curves back to the target.
  - Slightly lower, with more roll.
mistakes:
  - Aiming the face with your body — a push.
  - Flipping the hands, which hooks it.
fixes:
  - Set the face at the target first, then aim your body {right}.
  - Swing along your body line and let the setup create the curve.
club_choice: Consider one club less.
club_change: -1
drill: Start balls along an alignment stick on your body line.
related: [intentional-fade, crosswind, ball-above-feet, choosing-a-target]

---

Only try a shaped shot on the course once you can hit it reliably in practice. If in doubt,
aim for the widest safe area and play your normal shot.
