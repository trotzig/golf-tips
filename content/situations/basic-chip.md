---
title: Basic chip
summary: Short shot from just off the green — a little air, then roll like a putt to the hole.
category: short-game
tags: [chipping, short-game, ball-position, contact]
aliases: [chipping around the green, chip shot, short shot off the green]
level: beginner
clubs: [wedges, irons]
setup:
  - Take a narrow, slightly open stance.
  - Play the ball back of center.
  - Keep about 60% of your weight on your lead foot.
  - Set your hands slightly ahead of the ball.
swing:
  - Rock your shoulders with little wrist hinge.
  - Keep the same tempo back and through.
  - Let the loft lift the ball; don't scoop.
expect:
  - A low shot that lands on the green and rolls out.
mistakes:
  - Flicking the wrists to help the ball up.
  - Decelerating into the ball.
fixes:
  - Keep your wrists quiet and let the club's loft lift the ball.
  - Keep the same tempo back and through, finishing past the ball.
club_choice: Pick a landing spot just on the green, then the club that rolls the rest.
club_roll_chart: true
drill: Chip to land on a towel, using different clubs.
related: [pitch-shot, bump-and-run, chip-or-putt, tight-lie]
viz: basic-chip
updated: 2026-11-03
---

Most players lose more strokes around the green than anywhere else. A simple, repeatable chip is
the quickest way to lower scores.
