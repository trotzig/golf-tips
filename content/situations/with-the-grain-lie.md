setup:
  - Read the grass — shiny, light blades leaning toward the target mean you're with the grain.
  - Set up as normal, with the ball in its usual spot.
  - Pick a landing spot shorter than you would from a normal lie.
swing:
  - Make a smaller, smoother swing — the grass won't slow the club.
  - Brush the grass and let the club glide through.
expect:
  - The ball comes out faster and runs farther.
  - Less spin from the rough, a bit like a flyer.
  - Chips and putts from the fringe roll out more than they look.
mistakes:
  - Making your normal swing and running it well past the hole.
  - Hitting down hard and digging when a brush is enough.
club_choice: Less loft is fine for chips; from the rough, take one club less.
drill: Chip down-grain to a tee and find how short your landing spot needs to be.
advanced: On the green, putts down-grain roll faster and drift toward the way the grain grows.

With the grain, the blades lean toward the target, so the club slides over them instead of
pushing through. Contact feels easy and the ball comes off hot. Swing smaller, land it shorter and
expect plenty of roll, especially on firm, warm-season grass.
